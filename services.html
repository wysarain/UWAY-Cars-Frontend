Our Services

Uway Cars shows three main services. These are sample services for this student project.

1. Car Rental

A visitor can choose a car and rent it for one day or more. The cars are small, family and sedan cars. Sample prices are shown on the cars page.

2. Family Travel

Families can choose a car with enough seats and space for everyone. Cars like the Suzuki Wagon R and the Toyota Yaris are good for family trips.

3. Business Travel

People who travel for work can choose a comfortable car, like the Toyota Corolla. It is good for meetings, office visits and long trips.

How to Book a Car

  1. Look at the available cars on the cars page.
  2. Choose the car you like.
  3. Fill in the booking form.
