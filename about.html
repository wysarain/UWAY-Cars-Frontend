About Uway Cars

Uway Cars is a student project. It is not a real company, and it does not rent out real cars. All cars, prices and events on this website are sample data.

Purpose of the Website

The purpose of this website is to show how a car booking and car rental website can look. It has pages for cars, services, a photo gallery, events and two forms.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make car booking easy to understand. A visitor should be able to see the cars, choose one and fill in a simple form without any trouble.
