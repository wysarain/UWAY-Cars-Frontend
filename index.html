Welcome to Uway Cars

Uway Cars is a simple website for car booking and car rental. Here you can see different cars, read about our services and fill in a booking form.

Purpose of Website

This website is a student project. I am Awais Afzal and This is Web Engineering course at KFUEIT. Its purpose is to show how a car rental website can look and work.
